# Recurring payments

Recurring payments are charges that repeat on a schedule: rent, phone plans,
streaming services, gym memberships, cloud storage, software subscriptions. Each
one is small, but together they set a floor under your monthly spending that is
easy to lose track of.

## Why they deserve attention

Because they are automatic, recurring payments keep leaving your account whether
or not you are using the service. They also shape your cash flow: a forecast of
your balance is only as good as its list of known recurring items. Missing a
₹1,500 rent debit from the forecast makes every projection too optimistic.

## Keeping them under control

Review the list every couple of months. For each item ask: did I use this, and
would I sign up for it again today? Cancel the ones that fail. Where possible,
align renewal dates with just after your income lands, so a subscription never
lands on a low day.

## Recurring payments and a purchase decision

When you weigh a one-off purchase, the upcoming recurring debits matter as much
as the purchase itself. A purchase that looks fine against today's balance can
still push your projected minimum below your safety buffer once this month's
rent and subscriptions are taken into account.
